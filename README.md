# Supermarket Sales Data Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes supermarket sales data from Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt.
The aim is to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance,
and shopping patterns to help management make better business decisions.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab (Jupyter Notebook)

## Dataset
The analysis combines three supermarket sales datasets from Lagos, Abuja,
and Port Harcourt, containing 1,000 transactions and 17 original columns.

## Data Cleaning and Preparation
I combined the three datasets into one table.
I checked the column names, data types, missing values, and duplicate rows.
The dataset contained no missing values or duplicate rows.

## Analysis Performed
- Monthly sales performance
- Sales and gross income by branch
- Sales by product line and quantity sold
- Member and normal customer comparisons
- Sales by gender
- Payment methods
- Customer ratings
- Shopping patterns by hour
- Weekday and weekend sales
- Customer segmentation
- Relationship between ratings and spending

## Key Findings
- January recorded the highest monthly sales.
- Port Harcourt generated the highest total sales.
- Branch C generated the highest gross income.
- Food and Beverages generated the highest revenue.
- Members contributed slightly more total sales than normal customers.
- ePay was the most frequently used payment method.
- Weekdays generated more total sales, but weekends had a higher average transaction value.
- Customer ratings showed almost no linear relationship with transaction value.

## Business Recommendations
1. Keep popular products available to meet customer demand.
2. Encourage customer membership through loyalty rewards.
3. Introduce promotions during slower sales periods.
4. Provide enough staff during busy shopping hours.
5. Monitor branch performance and identify opportunities for improvement.
6. Maintain different payment options for customers.

## Limitations
- The dataset covers only three months.
- Complete operating expenses were not available.
- Detailed customer purchase histories were not available.

## Conclusion
This project shows how supermarket sales data can help management understand
business performance, customer behavior, and opportunities for improvement.
